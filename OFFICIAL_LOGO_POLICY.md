# Official Logo Policy

All official organization logos must be supplied image assets. Do not generate, redraw, trace, approximate, reconstruct, or replace official logos with CSS, SVG recreations, icon libraries, Canvas, or AI-generated artwork.

Official logo rendering must preserve the source asset exactly:

- Use the original uploaded image file.
- Use `object-fit: contain`.
- Preserve the source aspect ratio.
- Do not crop, stretch, recolor, retouch, or remove backgrounds that are part of the asset.
